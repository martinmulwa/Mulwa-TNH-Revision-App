# Clinical Exam Portal

The **Clinical Exam Portal** is a high-yield, distraction-free examination revision and self-assessment platform built specifically for medical, clinical, and nursing candidates. It provides structured exam practice, instant clinical rationale feedback, live performance tracking, and an automated mistake-correction queue.

---

## Features

### 1. Interactive Examination Workspace
* **Focus Mode Practice:** Clean, uncluttered question interface displaying clinical stems, structured answer options, and clear question index indicators.
* **Instant Rationale Feedback:** Comprehensive, evidence-based physiological and clinical explanations provided immediately upon answering, detailing why the correct option is valid and why distractors are contraindicated or inaccurate.
* **Immediate Chromatic Validation:** Visual confirmation highlighting correct answers in emerald styling and incorrect choices in alert crimson.
* **Bi-Directional Navigation:** Seamless navigation between previous and next questions within any active revision set, enabling candidates to review or reconsider answers before submission.
* **Question Bookmarking:** One-click flagging system allowing candidates to mark complex or high-yield stems for targeted follow-up.
* **Progress Bar:** Real-time visual indicator tracking completion status across the active revision set.

---

### 2. Dedicated Mistakes Review Queue
* **Automated Error Logging:** Questions answered incorrectly are instantly captured into a dedicated review queue.
* **Diagnostic Review Breakdown:** Displays the original question stem, the candidate's selected choice, the correct clinical answer, and the complete rationale explanation.
* **"Understood" Resolution Workflow:** Candidates can mark reviewed questions as understood once mastered, removing them from the active mistake queue and keeping revision focused on unmet learning needs.
* **Persistent Counter:** Live badge on the main dashboard showing the exact number of outstanding review items.

---

### 3. Performance Dashboard & Analytics
* **Paper-Level Metrics:** High-level summary cards reporting total exam papers attempted, fully completed papers, and average accuracy percentage across all attempted exams.
* **Quick Resume Card:** Prominently highlights the candidate's most recently active revision set with one-click resumption directly to the exact question where practice was paused.
* **Chronological Activity Log:** Detailed history feed documenting completed exam sets, score percentages, and timestamps.
* **Safe Progress Reset:** Protected "Clear Progress" tool with a modal confirmation safeguard to safely reset session scores and mistaken question logs when starting a new revision cycle.

---

### 4. Past Papers Archive & Categorization
* **Hierarchical Categorization:** Organized structure grouping past papers under academic blocks (e.g., Block 1) and specific clinical units (e.g., Respiratory Disorders).
* **Live Search & Filter:** Rapid keyword filtering searching across exam titles, academic units, and paper identifiers.
* **Multi-Criteria Sorting:** Quick toggle sorting by Alphabetical (A–Z), Reverse Alphabetical (Z–A), or default order.
* **Completion Badges:** Visual indicators displaying how many sets within each past paper have been completed.

---

### 5. Results & Performance Scorecard
* **Detailed Scorecard:** Immediate summary upon set completion displaying raw score, total attainable points, and calculated accuracy percentage.
* **Performance Banding:** Color-coded diagnostic grading evaluating mastery (Distinction, Credit, Pass, or Needs Review).
* **Actionable Next Steps:** Direct shortcuts to review mistaken stems, retake the current practice set, or navigate to other past papers.
* **Clipboard Answer Export:** Quick-copy utility formatting questions, chosen options, and scores into clean plain text for easy note-taking and revision logs.

---

### 6. Tab-Level Ephemeral Session Storage
* **Automatic Tab Persistence:** Exam progress, active answers, and performance history persist across page reloads and tab navigation via browser `sessionStorage`.
* **Zero Workstation Leakage:** Automatically clears when the browser tab is closed, ensuring complete data privacy on shared clinical laboratory and library computers.

---

### 7. Modern Ergonomics & Responsive Design
* **Clinical Typography:** Uniform, high-legibility typographic hierarchy utilizing Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter font families with generous vertical line spacing for comfortable prolonged reading.
* **Distraction-Free Visual Hierarchy:** Muted clinical slate surfaces, soft borders, and purposeful contrast ratios to minimize eye fatigue.
* **Cross-Device Layout:** Fully responsive layout with mobile-optimized touch targets, top navigation header, and intuitive bottom navigation bar.
